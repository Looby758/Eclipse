## What's new in Eclipse 1.0.2

**Smoother pages.** Opening a page no longer stutters. The pauses when you moved between pages are gone.

**Home**
- A new column on the right shows what's waiting for you, always open, instead of the old "For you tonight" menu.
- Hold Shift on Home to hide the dial and buttons, then click any poster to open it.
- The Spotlight poster wall is livelier: it mixes in recommendations, follows your pointer with a soft light, and opens a title when you click it.

**Actors and people**
- Click an actor to see who they played, with a Seen filter for the ones you've watched.
- Search for people as well as films and shows, and see the full cast of anything.
- On a show, see exactly which episodes someone was in.

**Recommendations that learn.** Mark suggestions as Interested or Not for me, and Eclipse gets better at picking for you.

**Noticing what you watch**
- The Chrome extension now tells Eclipse the exact episode you're watching on Disney+, NOW, Netflix and others.
- Watching with someone? Their episodes get ticked too.

**Discord status.** If you like, Discord can show what you're watching.

**Eclipse on your phone.** A phone page lets you check and tick things away from your computer.

### Updating the Chrome extension
The new extension comes with the app. Open Settings, scroll to Noticing what you watch, choose Show the extension folder, then in Chrome go to `chrome://extensions` and press the reload button on Eclipse. If you've never added it, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick that folder.

### Installing
- **Windows:** download `Eclipse-Setup-1.0.2.exe`. If you already have Eclipse, it updates itself.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, M1 or later):** download `Eclipse-1.0.2-arm64.dmg`.
- **Mac (Intel):** download `Eclipse-1.0.2-x64.dmg`.

On a Mac, the first time you open it, right-click Eclipse in Applications and choose Open, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate.

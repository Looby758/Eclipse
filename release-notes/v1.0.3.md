## What's new in Eclipse 1.0.3

**A new title page for the Spotlight look.** Open a film or show and everything is on one screen:
- The show's picture fills the window, with its logo, what's next and a Tick it button.
- A status menu, how many episodes are left and how long they'll take.
- A bar for every episode by its rating, without spoilers for the ones you haven't reached.
- The cast, with what else they've been in.
- A row of related titles from the same universe that are in your library, closest family first (WandaVision with Agatha All Along, Andor with Rogue One, and so on). Big universes like Marvel get a See all card.
- Continue now has a menu of every service the title is on, and remembers the one you pick.
- Choose Just the picture (or press P) to see the artwork on its own. Any click brings everything back.

Everything else opens in a frosted side panel. The Classic look keeps the old page.

**Better at noticing finished episodes**
- Eclipse now asks about an episode once you're in its last 10%, so it no longer misses ones where the credits or the next episode start early (NOW does this a lot).
- When the next episode plays on its own, Eclipse still asks about the one you just finished.
- Saying Not this to an old guess no longer stops Eclipse asking about that episode later, once it's sure you watched it.
- On NOW, Playing now no longer shows the wrong episode during the advert before the next one.
- Copy details in Settings now explains why an episode wasn't offered, if one ever goes missing.

### Updating the Chrome extension
This release includes extension 1.4.2. Open Settings, scroll to Noticing what you watch, choose Show the extension folder, then in Chrome go to `chrome://extensions` and press the reload button on Eclipse. If you've never added it, turn on Developer mode, choose Load unpacked, and pick that folder.

### Installing
- **Windows:** download `Eclipse-Setup-1.0.3.exe`. If you already have Eclipse, it updates itself.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, M1 or later):** download `Eclipse-1.0.3-arm64.dmg`.
- **Mac (Intel):** download `Eclipse-1.0.3-x64.dmg`.

On a Mac, the first time you open it, right-click Eclipse in Applications and choose Open, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate.

## What's new in Eclipse 1.1.1

**Small things that make every day easier**
- **Right-click a poster on Home** for a quick menu: Open, Watch soon, Mark watched, or Add to a list (tick existing lists or make a new one).
- **Back keeps your place.** Go back to Library, a list, Search or Settings and it scrolls to where you were, instead of the top.
- **Time left in this season.** A show's page now says how many episodes and how long are left in the season you're on ("4 episodes and 3h 10m left in season 3"), and roughly how long to finish the whole show. It uses real episode lengths when they're known.
- **Library by service.** A new Service picker beside Sort shows only what's on Netflix, Disney+ or whichever service you choose.
- **Trailer button** on title pages, when there's a trailer. It plays over the page and closes with Escape, the ✕ or a click outside.
- **Text size that sticks.** Ctrl + / − / 0 (⌘ on a Mac) or Ctrl and the mouse wheel change the text size, and Eclipse remembers it next time. Settings, Appearance also has Smaller, Normal, Larger and Largest.
- **Paste a link into search.** Paste a TMDB or IMDb link (or an IMDb id) and Eclipse opens that title straight away.
- **"Last watched 3 weeks ago"** under the cards in Library's Watching tab, so you can see what's gone quiet. It changes colour after 60 days.
- **Double-click to tick** now works on the rating bars on a show's page and in the heatmap, as it already did on the episode dots.

**On your phone:** For you now picks recommendations the same way the computer does, learning from what you've said you're interested in or not, and each card says why it was picked.

**Fixes**
- Watching a YouTube video named after a film (a trailer called "Avengers: Doomsday", say) no longer counts as watching the film. Only streaming services count now.
- Home's poster wall always fills the window, even when you zoom out.

### Installing
- **Windows:** download `Eclipse-Setup-1.1.1.exe`. If you already have Eclipse, it updates itself.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, M1 or later):** download `Eclipse-1.1.1-arm64.dmg`.
- **Mac (Intel):** download `Eclipse-1.1.1-x64.dmg`.

On a Mac, the first time you open it, right-click Eclipse in Applications and choose Open, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate.

The Chrome extension hasn't changed since 1.0.3, so there's nothing to reload.

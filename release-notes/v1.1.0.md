## What's new in Eclipse 1.1.0

**An opening.** When Eclipse starts, the eclipse rises on a dark screen, then your posters fly out of it onto the wall. It takes about two and a half seconds, and any key or click skips it.

**Finishing a show is an event.** Tick the last episode of a show that has ended and the screen goes dark for a short trailer of your time with it, set to the show's own theme tune: when you started, who you watched it with, your longest night, your longest break and its best episode. It ends with every season's poster and a *COMPLETE* card you can copy or save to share.

**Premiere night.** On the day a season you've been waiting for comes out, Spotlight Home turns into that show, with a countdown to the UK release time on its service. When it's out, one click starts it. If the time is wrong, Not the right time? lets you fix it, and Usual Home for today puts things back.

**The Shelf.** A new page with three parts:
- **Finished:** a shelf for each year, with a box set for everything you've watched all the way through. Pull one out to see the back of the box: your dates, hours, who you watched it with, the best episode and the credits.
- **Coming soon:** announced seasons and films you're waiting for sit shrink-wrapped with a date sticker, and unwrap on release day.
- **Favourites wall:** add up to 30 favourites from any title page and drag them into your own order.

**Milestones and a Stats page.** When a tick takes you past a milestone ("That was your 1,000th episode", "100 hours of The Walking Dead"), a small note slides in. The new Stats page shows your totals, records, streaks, charts, top shows and films, genres, decades and every milestone with how far you are from the next.

**How did it hit you?** After you tick an episode, tap an emoji to say how it felt. The episode ratings grid now has a Mine view beside TMDB's, which shows where you and everyone else disagree. Your reactions also help Eclipse pick recommendations.

**Star maps.** Watch orders like Marvel, Star Wars and The Walking Dead now have a Map view: each series is a constellation, lit up by what you've seen. Scroll past the cast on a show's page and the background turns into its part of the sky.

**Make it yours**
- Change poster and Change background on a title page let you pick your own artwork, used everywhere.
- Rate films and shows with five stars, in halves.
- Clicking a poster now zooms into its page, and Back shrinks it home.
- Your journey on a show's page shows each watch season by season on the calendar, with the long breaks marked.
- Ticking feels better: the dots fill like ink and a progress ring jumps.
- Optional sounds (Settings, Appearance, Sounds): a soft click when you tick and a short chime when you finish a season. Off unless you turn them on.

*Keep the poster wall still* in Settings tones down all the new animation.

### Installing
- **Windows:** download `Eclipse-Setup-1.1.0.exe`. If you already have Eclipse, it updates itself.
- **Mac (Apple Silicon, M1 or later):** download `Eclipse-1.1.0-arm64.dmg`.
- **Mac (Intel):** download `Eclipse-1.1.0-x64.dmg`.

On a Mac, the first time you open it, right-click Eclipse in Applications and choose Open, because it isn't signed with a paid Apple certificate.

The Chrome extension hasn't changed since 1.0.3, so there's nothing to reload.
